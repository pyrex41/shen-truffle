# Provenance

Canonical Shen 42.0 KLambda modules are vendored byte-for-byte from the
`pyrex41/shen-upstream` mirror, tag `s42-pristine-20260825`, commit
`28825a211f5b2fb952510dab17267ca8eb9594a0` (archive SHA-256
`30abdc7e5a1e27b7a20109c1ed141e4712885e31f24d9710d16415fbbd4dfb23`).

The 15 upstream modules are loaded in the order required by Tarver's S42
distribution. `extension-*.kl` files are community additions and remain
separate from the canonical inventory.
